🧠 Algorithm: how it works

The clock

"Awake for" counts from the end of the last nap or night sleep, whichever ended most recently. While a nap or night is running it switches to "Asleep for".

What "past 7 days" means

The 7 full days before today. Today never counts towards its own averages. Days with nothing logged are skipped, and a suggestion needs at least one day of data (otherwise it says "no data yet").

Nap suggestions

For each nap number (1st, 2nd, 3rd…) the app averages the start time and the length of that nap over the past 7 days. The number of naps planned is your "Target naps/day", or more if you have logged more.

Wake-up

Shown as the average wake-up time of the past 7 nights, or the real time once this morning's night sleep has ended.

Nap time target

The average total nap time per day over the past 7 days. Night sleep is not counted.

Catching up after short naps

Naps logged today plus the suggested remaining naps are compared with the target. If they fall short, the missing time is added to the remaining naps, in proportion to how long each usually is (shown as "+20m"). A nap is never stretched beyond 3 hours, so on a very short day the target may not be reached. Longer-than-usual naps do not shorten later ones. A nap that is running counts until its usual end, or until now if it is already longer.

Awake window limits

Every awake window, from wake-up to the first nap, between naps, and before bedtime, is kept between 30 minutes and 3h 30m. A suggested nap never starts more than 3h 30m after the previous sleep ended. If the averages would give a longer wait, the nap is moved earlier.

Bedtime

Your target bedtime is the goal. The estimate lands exactly on it whenever the naps allow, and it can move up to 30 minutes either way if that is needed to respect the awake limits. If that is still not enough, the naps still to come are moved together, as little as possible, earlier or later than usual (the plan says by how much). If there are too few naps to stay under 3h 30m awake until bedtime, bedtime is moved earlier instead and the plan suggests adding a nap.
